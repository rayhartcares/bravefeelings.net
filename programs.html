bravefeelings.com

Standalone Mini-Program

Confidence Spark

A complete, 30-page interactive program to help children build lasting, authentic confidence from the inside out.

What Confidence Spark Does

Confidence Spark takes children on a guided journey to discover the strength that's already inside them. Through interactive activities, gentle storytelling, and encouraging prompts, children learn to challenge negative self-talk, recognize their own strengths, and develop a more secure, stable sense of who they are.

This is not a quick fix — it's a thoughtful, layered experience designed to leave a lasting impression on how a child sees themselves.

What Children Gain

  • A stronger, kinder inner voice
  • Healthier, more realistic self-talk
  • Genuine belief in their own abilities
  • Tools for moments of self-doubt
  • A completed experience they're proud of

Standalone Mini-Program

Worry Shrinker

Helping children understand worry, recognize how it shows up, and learn to quiet anxious thoughts.

Worry Shrinker helps children explore why worry happens, what it feels like in the body, and how to work with it rather than against it. Children learn that worry is normal — and that there are practical, gentle strategies to help them feel more in control.

  • Understand what worry is and where it comes from
  • Recognize physical signals of anxiety
  • Practice calming thought strategies
  • Build a sense of control over worried feelings
Get Worry Shrinker →
⛈️
Standalone Mini-Program

Calm the Storm

Helping children recognize rising anger, pause before reacting, and respond with calm and intention.

Calm the Storm gives children an honest, practical look at anger — what triggers it, what it does in the body, and how to interrupt the escalation cycle before it takes over. Children walk away with real strategies they can use in the moment.

  • Identify anger warning signs early
  • Understand the anger escalation cycle
  • Practice pause-and-reset strategies
  • Choose calm, constructive responses
