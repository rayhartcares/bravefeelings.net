Standalone Mini-Programs

Focused Programs for Specific Challenges

Not every child needs the complete program right away. These standalone mini-programs target specific emotional needs — each one a complete, independent experience.

★ Featured
✨
Standalone Mini-Program

Confidence Spark

Confidence Spark is a complete, 30-page interactive program that guides children through building lasting confidence from the inside out. Through gentle prompts, guided activities, and encouraging storytelling, children develop a stronger inner voice, healthier self-talk, and real belief in themselves.

  • Builds authentic self-belief and inner voice
  • Develops positive, realistic self-talk
  • Interactive and child-led experience
  • Grounded in evidence-based frameworks
  • Ideal for ages 4–12
Get Confidence Spark →

Standalone Mini-Program

Worry Shrinker

Worry Shrinker helps children understand where worry comes from, recognize how it shows up in their body, and practice gentle strategies to calm their thoughts and feel more in control.

  • Understand what worry really is
  • Recognize anxiety signals in the body
  • Calm worried thoughts with guided tools
Get Worry Shrinker →
⛈️
Standalone Mini-Program

Calm the Storm

Calm the Storm helps children recognize rising anger before it takes over — and gives them real, practical strategies to pause, reset, and respond more calmly instead of reacting.

  • Recognize anger warning signs early
  • Practice pausing before reacting
  • Learn calm, constructive responses
Get Calm the Storm →
